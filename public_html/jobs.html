Lake Patrol Position Open

Seasonal position, boating season is just around the corner.

Facebook update posted February 23, 2026: We’re hiring. We are looking for a few individuals that are interested in Lake Patrol. $20 an hour. Hours per week are negotiable. If you are interested, please email lslclerk@hotmail.com.

Little Swan Lake (LSL) is located 22 miles northeast of Macomb and 4 miles west of Avon, Illinois. This is a part-time position that typically runs from May through October. Patrolman average 10 to 20 hours per week, with most patrolling during the highest activity times, especially weekends and holidays. Weekday hours are also required.

$20 an hour

Qualifications

  • 21 years of age or older
  • Good people skills required
  • Familiar with Illinois Department of Natural Resources rules
  • Familiar with Little Swan Lake club rules and regulations
  • Able to work a flexible schedule
  • Ability to operate watercraft
  • Good references
  • Ability to be bonded
  • Little Swan Lake member in good standing preferred

Schedule Notes

Hours per week are negotiable, but coverage is most important during weekends, holidays, and other high-traffic lake times.

How to Apply

If you are interested, please email lslclerk@hotmail.com.
