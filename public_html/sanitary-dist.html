Sanitary District (Water) Information

Updated May 2025

Meeting Dates: The Sanitary District Board meets at the LSL Clubhouse at 7:00 PM on the second Wednesday of each month. Meetings are open to the public.

Please note that the Sanitary District is not an entity or part of Little Swan Lake Club. Questions and requests should be directed to the Sanitary District Board. This page is provided to make Sanitary District information easier for lake owners to find.
