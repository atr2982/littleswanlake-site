View Full Tournament Details

Entry & Payout

  • $100 per team
  • Optional $10 Big Bass side pot (largest bass wins)
  • 50% of entry fees donated to Little Swan Lake Fishing
  • Remaining 50% paid to top 3 teams

Team Limits & Pools

  • Teams consist of 2 people
  • Spots are limited to first 15 paid teams
  • 15 teams x $100 = $1,500 total entry fees
  • 50% donated to Little Swan Lake Fishing: $750
  • Payout pool (remaining 50%): $750

Top 3 Team Payouts (50/30/20)

Place Percentage Amount
1st Place 50% $375
2nd Place 30% $225
3rd Place 20% $150

Optional Big Bass Side Pot

  • Paid at boat check day of tournament
  • $10 per team x 15 teams = $150 pot (winner take all)

Tournament Rules

  • Observe and obey all state fishing and boating laws
  • One member from each team must use a boat with an LSL boat sticker and membership card
  • Limited to first 15 paid teams (checks mailed and received by Little Swan Lake)
  • Aerated livewell required and inspected before launch
  • Pre-tournament meeting at 6:45 AM
  • 20 mph lake speed limit for safety
  • No alcohol during the tournament
  • Obey all lake rules, including no-wake areas
  • Launch order drawn at meeting
  • Life jackets required at takeoff
  • Boats launch 15 seconds apart
  • 14 inch minimum length
  • 3 bass limit per team
  • Only 3 bass max in livewell (cull immediately)
  • Stay 75 ft from other boats and off-limit areas
  • Do not cut off other anglers fishing ahead
  • 1 lb penalty for each dead or non-releasable fish (determined by tournament director)
  • 1 lb penalty for short fish
  • Use non-intrusive culling bulbs
  • Disqualification for late check-in at 1:00 PM (no exceptions)
  • Big bass breaks all final weight ties
  • Big bass tie is broken by overall weight (coin flip if still tied)
  • Little Swan Lake is not responsible for accidents
  • Share rides to save parking at ramp
